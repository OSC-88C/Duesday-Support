Help with Duesday

Budget, spending and goals

Use Money to set a monthly budget, record other spending, create savings goals and choose how much of the month's budget left to allocate to each goal. Duesday does not connect to a bank or move money.

Adding subscriptions

Use Add to choose a common service, enter details manually, or import a screenshot of your Apple subscriptions. Review every detected name, price, billing cycle and renewal date before saving.

Editing, pausing or deleting

Open Money, choose Spending, then select Search and manage subscriptions. Paused subscriptions remain stored but are excluded from current totals and reminders.

Notifications

Open iPhone Settings, select Duesday, then Notifications. Duesday schedules reminders locally on your device.

Purchases

Open Duesday Settings and select Duesday Pro. Use Restore Purchases if you previously subscribed with the same Apple Account. Apple handles billing and cancellations.

Accounts and deletion

An account is optional and is used for verified referrals. Delete it from Duesday Settings. Your local subscriptions, spending, budgets and goals remain on the device until you clear local data or remove the app.

Support and feature requests

Email hello@dakotatechsg.com, or use Email support and Request a feature in Duesday Settings.
