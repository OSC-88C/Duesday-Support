Help with Duesday

Adding subscriptions

Use Add to enter a subscription manually, choose a common service, or import a screenshot. Review any details detected from an image before saving.

Editing, pausing or deleting

Open Subscriptions, then use the menu beside an item. Paused subscriptions remain stored but are excluded from current totals and reminders.

Notifications

Open iPhone Settings, select Duesday, then Notifications. Duesday schedules reminders locally on your device.

Purchases

Open Duesday Settings and select Duesday Pro. Use Restore Purchases if you previously subscribed with the same Apple Account. Apple handles billing and cancellations.

Accounts and deletion

An account is optional and is used for verified referrals. Delete it from Duesday Settings. Your local subscription list remains on the device until you clear local data or remove the app.
